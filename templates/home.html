◎

XOR Bucket Diversity

Guarantees at least one connection per Kademlia bucket, so attacker IDs clustered near the victim can't dominate every slot.

▦

Subnet Capping

Limits how many connections can come from the same simulated subnet — attackers rarely control as many real machines as fake IDs.

⏱

Trust-Weighted Gating

New peers are capped to a small share of connection slots each round, forcing attackers to sustain presence — and risk detection — over time.
